# 01: Realized Profit & Loss (P&L) Engine, Cost Snapshots & Executive Command Center Dashboard (Tab 1)

**What to build:** Salon owners and branch managers can open the Executive Financial & Operations Command Center on the main administrative dashboard, filter by branch and date-range presets (`Hôm nay`, `7 ngày qua`, `Tháng này`, or a custom `Từ ngày – Đến ngày` range), and inspect the Realized Profit & Loss (P&L) report (**Tab 1: Tổng quan & P&L Thực tế**). Every stock movement (`SERVICE_CONSUMPTION`, `RETAIL_SALE`, `STOCK_IN`, `STOCK_ADJUSTMENT`) automatically records an immutable snapshot of the product's unit cost and total cost at transaction time so future purchase price updates never distort historical margins. For each `PAID` `Order`, order-level discounts are pro-rated proportionally between service items and retail `Master Product` items, and the system computes `Chemical COGS` (per `ml`/`g` consumed), retail COGS, staff commissions from the `Commission Ledger`, Service Net Profit, Retail Net Profit, and combined `Order Net Profit`. The Executive Dashboard displays two-tier P&L summary cards (**Tier 1: Operational Order Net Profit** from completed orders and **Tier 2: Net Profit After Fixed Payroll** after subtracting active staff `Base Salary`), an interactive daily Revenue vs. Cost vs. Net Profit trend chart, and a per-order P&L breakdown table, pre-populated with a rich historical seed dataset from the V5 database migration.
