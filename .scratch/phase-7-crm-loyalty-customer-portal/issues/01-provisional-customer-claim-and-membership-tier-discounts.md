# 01: Provisional Customer In-Place Claim, Spend-Based Membership Tier Engine & Automatic POS/Booking Discounts

**What to build:** Returning walk-in or unauthenticated booking customers who previously provided their phone number at the salon (`Provisional Customer`) can register an online login with that same phone number and seamlessly claim their existing customer profile in-place—immediately inheriting their past `Bookings`, `Orders`, `Visual Logs`, and `Cumulative Spend` without receiving a duplicate-account error. Every identified customer's `Cumulative Spend` (`total_spent`) is automatically tracked across all completed `PAID` `Orders` using full net order revenue ($\text{subtotal} - \text{effectiveDiscount}$, including upfront `Deposits`) and mapped to one of four `Membership Tiers`: `STANDARD` ($< 2,000,000\text{ VND}$, $0\%$), `SILVER` ($\ge 2,000,000\text{ VND}$, $5\%$), `GOLD` ($\ge 5,000,000\text{ VND}$, $10\%$), and `DIAMOND` ($\ge 10,000,000\text{ VND}$, $15\%$). When checking in a `Booking`, creating a `Walk-in Order`, or adding/removing items on an unpaid `Order`, the system automatically calculates and pre-fills the customer's `Membership Tier` discount into the order's discount amount while keeping catalog item prices intact so staff commissions in the `Commission Ledger` (`ADR-0009`) are never reduced. Receptionists can still manually override the order discount at POS (`is_discount_manual = true`), and customers and staff see `Membership Tier` badges and expected member savings across the Customer Portal header, `Booking` wizard summary step, and POS `Order` drawer.
