# 02: Customer Portal Workspace (My Bookings & Membership Card), 2-Hour Online Cancellation & Retained Deposit Offset

**What to build:** Logged-in customers can navigate to their personal Customer Portal workspace (`/customer/profile`) to manage their appointments (**Tab 1: Lịch hẹn của tôi**) and inspect their loyalty status and benefits (**Tab 3: Hạng thành viên & Quyền lợi**). On the **Lịch hẹn của tôi** tab, customers view all upcoming and historical `Bookings` and can cancel any `PENDING_PAYMENT` or `CONFIRMED` `Booking` online when at least 2 hours remain before the scheduled start time. Cancelling online immediately releases any active Redis `Slot Locks` for the stylist, transitions the `Booking` to `CANCELLED`, and—if the 20% `Deposit` was already paid—credits the full `Deposit` amount into the customer's `Retained Deposit` balance (`credit_balance`) with deposit status `CREDITED`. When a customer with a positive `Retained Deposit` balance creates a new `Booking` whose required 20% `Deposit` is covered by their `Retained Deposit`, the system automatically deducts the credit, records a `CREDIT_BALANCE` `Payment Transaction`, and confirms the `Booking` (`CONFIRMED`, `depositStatus = PAID`) immediately without requiring a VietQR transfer; any remaining `Retained Deposit` balance is also automatically applied to `deposit_deducted` when checking in or creating a `Walk-in Order` at POS.

**Blocked by:** 01: Provisional Customer In-Place Claim, Spend-Based Membership Tier Engine & Automatic POS/Booking Discounts

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Authenticated customer portal endpoints return the customer's loyalty/credit summary (`membershipTier`, `discountPercent`, `totalSpent`, `nextTier`, `amountToNextTier`, `creditBalance`) and their chronological list of `Bookings` enriched with `canCancelOnline` (`true` when status is `PENDING_PAYMENT` or `CONFIRMED` and start time is $\ge 2$ hours away).
- [ ] Cancelling a `Booking` online as a customer succeeds when $\ge 2$ hours remain before `bookingTime`—releasing Redis `Slot Locks`, setting status to `CANCELLED`, and (when `depositStatus == PAID`) adding `depositAmount` to `credit_balance` (`Retained Deposit`) with `depositStatus = CREDITED`—and is rejected with a clear validation error when $< 2$ hours remain.
- [ ] Creating a new `Booking` for an identified customer whose `Retained Deposit` (`credit_balance`) is $\ge$ the required 20% `Deposit` automatically deducts `depositAmount` from `credit_balance`, records a `SUCCESS` `Payment Transaction` (`paymentMethod = 'CREDIT_BALANCE'`), and transitions the `Booking` directly to `CONFIRMED` (`depositStatus = PAID`) without requiring a VietQR transfer; checking in a `Booking` or creating a `Walk-in Order` at POS automatically applies any remaining `credit_balance` into `deposit_deducted`.
- [ ] The Customer Portal (`/customer/profile`) renders **Tab 1 (Lịch hẹn của tôi)** with upcoming/past bookings, 2-hour policy notices, and a confirmation modal for online cancellation, and **Tab 3 (Hạng thành viên & Quyền lợi)** with an interactive VIP membership card, `Cumulative Spend` progress bar toward the next tier, `Retained Deposit` balance callout, and 4-tier benefit matrix.
- [ ] The `Booking` wizard detects when a newly created `Booking` was immediately confirmed via `Retained Deposit` (`status == CONFIRMED` and `depositStatus == PAID`) and presents the confirmed reservation summary directly without requiring a VietQR scan.
- [ ] Automated backend integration tests verify the 2-hour cancellation rule ($\ge 2\text{h}$ accepted, $< 2\text{h}$ rejected), Redis `Slot Lock` release, `Retained Deposit` crediting, and automatic `CREDIT_BALANCE` booking confirmation and POS `deposit_deducted` offset, and all frontend components stay strictly $\le 250$ LOC.
