# 03: Customer Beauty History (`Visual Log` Gallery), Post-Visit `Service Review` & Stylist Star Ratings Across Booking & Admin KPIs

**What to build:** Logged-in customers visiting the Customer Portal (`/customer/profile`) can open **Tab 2: Lịch sử Làm đẹp & Hồ sơ Kỹ thuật** to inspect all of their completed `PAID` `Orders`, including performed services, net amount paid, participating stylists, and a personal transformation gallery showing before/after photographs, hair/skin condition notes, and care/formula summaries from each visit's `Visual Log` (while keeping internal `actual_bom_json` chemical inventory consumption private to salon staff). For each completed `PAID` `Order` containing at least one salon service, the customer can submit a one-time `Service Review` (1-to-5 star rating and comment) via an interactive review modal that pre-selects the order's Primary Stylist (the stylist with the highest earned commission weight across the Order's `Step Assignments`) and allows choosing any stylist who worked on that `Order`. Submitted ratings immediately update each stylist's average star rating (`★`) and total review count on the stylist selection cards in the `Booking` wizard and in the **Hiệu suất & Lương Nhân sự** KPI table on the Executive Admin Dashboard.

**Blocked by:** 02: Customer Portal Workspace (My Bookings & Membership Card), 2-Hour Online Cancellation & Retained Deposit Offset
