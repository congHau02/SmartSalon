# 01: Stylist Quick Service Ticket, Daily Worklog & End-of-Shift Cash/Transfer Handover

**What to build:** At salon branches without a dedicated receptionist (`STAFF`) or on days when the receptionist is off-duty, stylists (`STYLIST`) and staff on the `/pos` workspace can open the **"Phiếu cắt & Tổng kết ngày"** (`Stylist Daily Worklog`) view to independently record completed services, settle customer payments at the chair, and reconcile their daily ticket tally and cash-in-hand at shift end. For walk-in customers, a stylist can submit a 1-step `Quick Service Ticket`—selecting performed services (and optional retail products), entering customer phone/name (or quick walk-in guest), and choosing a payment method (`CASH`, `VIETQR` with live dynamic QR preview, or `CARD`). For scheduled appointments, the stylist can view their upcoming assigned `Bookings` for the day directly inside the worklog and click **"Nhận khách & Chốt phiếu"** to check in the booking, apply any paid 20% `Deposit`, and settle the remaining balance in a single action. Both quick-ticket flows execute atomically on the canonical POS `Order` pipeline: assigning 100% of default `Service Steps` to the executing stylist, applying any eligible customer `Membership Tier` discount and `Retained Deposit` credit, deducting standard `BOM` chemical consumables and retail units from `Branch Inventory`, recording the `Payment Transaction`, writing immutable `Commission Ledger` entries, and stamping the settling user (`settled_by_user_id` and `settled_at`) on the `PAID` `Order`. The `Stylist Daily Worklog` aggregates the stylist's daily completed ticket count (`Orders`), completed `Service Steps`, total service revenue performed, total earned commission, itemized ticket list, and a collector-scoped **Đối soát tiền thu hộ cuối ca** breakdown separating `CASH` in hand (to hand over to the manager) and `VIETQR`/`CARD` transfers on orders personally settled by that stylist from orders settled by a receptionist at the counter.
