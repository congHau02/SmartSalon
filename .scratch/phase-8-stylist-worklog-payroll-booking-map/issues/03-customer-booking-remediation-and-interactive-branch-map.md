# 03: Customer Online Booking Wizard Remediation & Interactive Branch Map with Geolocation Directions

**What to build:** Customers visiting the online `Booking` wizard (`/customer/booking`) can seamlessly complete all four reservation steps from branch discovery to 5-minute Redis `Slot Lock` and VietQR `Deposit` checkout. In Step 1 (Chọn Chi nhánh & Dịch vụ), customers can explore all active SmartSalon branches on an interactive OpenStreetMap/Leaflet map alongside the branch selection cards: clicking a branch card smoothly pans/zooms the map (`flyTo`) to that salon's pin and opens its info popup, while clicking a map pin selects that branch for the booking. Customers can click **"Gần tôi nhất"** to compute the real-time Haversine distance in kilometers (`Cách bạn X.X km`) from their browser geolocation to each salon and automatically select/sort the closest branch, or click **"Chỉ đường"** on any branch card or map popup to launch turn-by-turn Google Maps directions (`google.com/maps/dir`) to the salon. Advancing to Step 2 (Chọn giờ & Stylist) renders all 30-minute `Slots` across Morning, Afternoon, and Evening groups using normalized slot fields (`time` and `status`) without runtime crashes, and advancing to Step 3 (Xác nhận thông tin) as either a guest or an authenticated member cleanly submits the customer's `fullName` and `phone` to lock the slot and reach Step 4.
